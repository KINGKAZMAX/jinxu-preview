JinXu Song / JinXuSong-Regular

This Regular WOFF2 subset is derived from Adobe Source Han Serif CN
2.003R (SubsetOTF/CN/SourceHanSerifCN-Regular.otf). It covers the
checked-in exhibition catalogue and interface, plus all printable ASCII.
The subset has its own family and PostScript names and preserves the
upstream copyright and SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices. Basic
kerning, localized forms and vertical alternates are retained.
